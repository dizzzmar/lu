# Design system
Siena-inspired immersive cinema gallery, translated into a warm, open identity for Lucía. Experience mode: media leads; interface stays sparse.
## Tokens
Background #f4f0e8; ink #302c28; accent #ad432d; line #d5cfc4. Self-hosted Bodoni Moda for expressive editorial display and Manrope for navigation and information. 28px desktop page gutter; 14px on mobile. Film frames radius 24px. No invented projects, awards, client attribution or email.
## Composition and behavior
Fixed typographic identity and ticket-shaped film selector/menu. Large cinematic placeholder frames; project titles and metadata anchor lower left. Vertical wheel/touch scroll and pointer dragging traverse gallery. Film index jumps to frames. Explore opens an explicitly labeled placeholder detail. About/contact are accessible dialog surfaces with keyboard dismissal and managed focus. No intro gate. Reduced-motion disables parallax and smooth scrolling.
## Assets
Local, generic Unsplash landscapes are temporary visual placeholders, not Lucía's films. Sources recorded in docs/research/siena/README.md. No Siena film media, fonts, logos or production bundles are reused.
